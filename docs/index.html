Track: General

For everyone. Protecting yourself, your accounts and your data, with no technical background needed. Take them in order.

  1. 01

    Passwords and Accounts, done properly

    BeginnerAbout 70 minutes

    How accounts actually get broken into, and the few habits that stop almost all of it: unique passwords, password managers, two-factor codes (scan a live one with your phone), passkeys and recovery. Ends with a checklist you can do tonight.

    Watch first: Crypto 101

    Read as one page

  2. 02

    How HTTPS Really Works

    BeginnerAbout 90 minutes

    Follow one web request from the address bar to the padlock: DNS, what café Wi-Fi can see, a real TLS 1.3 handshake you can step through, certificates and their warnings, and what still leaks. The natural sequel to Crypto 101.

    Watch first: Crypto 101

    Read as one page

  3. 03

    Phishing and Scams in the Age of AI

    BeginnerAbout 60 minutes

    Scams no longer have bad spelling. The five pressure tricks every scam uses, how to read a link without clicking it, why voice clones and deepfakes call for a call-back rule, and what to do in the first hour if you're caught.

    Watch first: Passwords and Accounts

    Read as one page

  4. 04

    Your Phone, Safely

    BeginnerAbout 55 minutes

    Your phone holds your bank, your email and your two-factor codes. What your passcode and face unlock each protect, what apps can see, fake apps and updates, SIM swaps, and what to do the moment it's lost, with a checklist to tick on your own phone.

    Watch first: Passwords and Accounts

    Read as one page

  5. 05

    Securing Your Home Network

    BeginnerAbout 70 minutes

    Your router, smart TV and doorbell camera are part of your security. Fix a mock router, sort your devices onto guest and smart networks, see what your provider can see, and leave with a 30-minute checklist.

    Watch first: How HTTPS Really Works

    Read as one page

  6. 06

    Backups and Ransomware

    BeginnerAbout 55 minutes

    Ransomware locks your files and asks for money; dead disks and slips of the finger do the same damage for free. Sync is not backup: build a 3-2-1 plan, see how far back you can go, and rehearse the first hour.

    Watch first: Phishing and Scams in the Age of AI

    Read as one page

  7. 07

    Privacy Tools Explained

    BeginnerAbout 80 minutes

    VPNs, Tor, encrypted messages and encrypted backups all "protect your privacy". See who each one hides you from, who you trust instead, and where a backup quietly undoes it.

    Watch first: How HTTPS Really Works

    Read as one page

  8. 08

    Modern Messaging Protocols

    IntermediateAbout 90 minutes

    How Signal-style apps start a private chat when your friend is offline, survive a stolen key, and cope with groups and quantum computers. Real X25519 and a live Double Ratchet you can attack, in your browser.

    Watch first: Crypto 101; Privacy Tools Explained helps

    Read as one page

  9. 09

    Zero-Knowledge Proofs

    AdvancedAbout 95 minutes

    How do you prove you know a secret, or that a statement is true, without revealing anything else? Walk the cave, run a real Schnorr proof, watch Fiat-Shamir go wrong, and try an over-18 check that never shows your birthday.

    Watch first: Crypto 101; Modern Messaging Protocols helps

    Cloud Security Fundamentals

    IntermediateAbout 90 minutes
